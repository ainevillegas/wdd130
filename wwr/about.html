White Water Rafting (WWR)

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide exciting, safe, and memorable white-water rafting adventures for individuals and groups while promoting respect for river environments and outdoor recreation.

History

Our rafting company began when a small group of river enthusiasts decided to turn a shared passion into a business. In the early days, they guided friends and neighbors using borrowed boats and homemade gear.

Today, the company offers guided trips for families, schools, and experienced paddlers while supporting conservation and responsible recreation.
