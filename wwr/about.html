History

Our rafting company began when a small group of river enthusiasts decided to turn a shared passion into a business. In the early days, they led friends and neighbors down nearby rivers using borrowed boats and homemade gear. As demand grew, the founders invested in professional equipment, safety training, and experienced guides.

Today, the company offers guided trips for families, schools, and experienced paddlers. Strong partnerships with conservation groups help protect river access while promoting responsible outdoor recreation.
