Philippines

The Philippines is an archipelago located in Southeast Asia, consisting of over 7,000 islands. It is known for its beaches, biodiversity, and rich culture. With a rich history influenced by Indigenous communities and later Spanish and American colonization, the country blends tradition and modern life in everything from local festival to major cities like Manila, Cebu and Davao.
